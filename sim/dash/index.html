A 12-month Monte Carlo simulation (300 paths per run) using the fee splits, curve maths and risk limits from Ringside's programs. Adoption levels are assumptions, not forecasts. Middle outcome shown, with the 10th–90th percentile range.

What launching $RING brings in on day one

$RING launches on its own curve like any other token, with the treasury as its creator. The money people pay into the curve does not come to the treasury: when the curve sells out it opens a Raydium pool and the LP tokens are burned.

The team buy's value is on paper only: selling it would move the pool's price against $RING holders, and it is locked for 7 days by the launch program. It is not capital.

How money reaches the vault after that

1. Every launch on Ringside

0.02 SOL launch fee, 0.5% protocol fee on curve trades, 1.5 SOL when a token graduates. This SOL revenue is the main source.

2. Swap and deposit

The treasury sweep swaps the share of SOL revenue you choose to USDC and deposits it. The vault mints treasury LP shares.

3. Perps fees compound

10% of every perps fee is the protocol's; the keeper turns it into treasury LP shares hourly. 60% goes to all LPs.

4. $RING holders

Get no yield and no claim on the vault. The shares stay with the treasury.

Why a bigger vault mostly sits idle

Perps on Ringside are capped per market at 1.5% of the SOL behind the price, to make price manipulation unprofitable. That is about — per side on a new curve, — near graduation and — on a graduated token. A vault of about — already gives every market a risk budget that covers a typical day's trader win.

So perps volume is set by the caps and by how many tokens are live, not by the vault. Money swept in beyond that adds safety, but it doesn't add trading, and the LPs' yield falls as the vault grows. That is the trade-off the table below shows.

Assumptions and method

From the code: 30 virtual SOL and 1,073M virtual tokens on a mainnet curve, 800M tokens sold on the curve, curve fees 0.5% protocol / 0.3% creator / 0.2% insurance, 0.02 SOL launch fee, 1.5 SOL graduation fee, perps fees 0.30% (new tokens) and 0.15% (graduated), fee split 60% LPs / 10% protocol / 10% keeper / 10% creator / 10% insurance, risk budget 0.25% and 1% of the vault per market, at most 40 live markets on new tokens.
